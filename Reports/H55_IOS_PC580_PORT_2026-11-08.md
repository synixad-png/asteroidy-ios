## Delivered on this branch
1. W3 HANGAR routes into native SceneKit H55ScenePage580; MENU returns.
2. Only this exact GLB is accepted on iOS (hash+size+semantic checks).
   It is loaded from bundled resources if available, or imported once from
   iOS Files and persisted in Application Support. Never fall back to old
   GpuHangar91/99 or procedural placeholder.
3. Version-pinned GLTFKit2 (0.5.15) loads glTF geometry, materials,
   skeleton and animation tracks. It is a third-party *decoder*, not
   another game source.
4. Initial camera at PC v580 HubWalk375 position (0,1.80,12.5), yaw
   aligned with the +Z source heading.
5. PC H55 idle loops for rotors/repair arms/lights and mechanic idle
   and a menu to select source animation clips.
6. Original 24 H46 light proxies from GLB metadata are converted to
   SceneKit lights. SceneKit light units differ from PC OpenGL.
7. CI: Linux GLB validation and anti-legacy regression; Apple iOS
   native source compilation; code-quality checks.
